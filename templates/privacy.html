Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ABC pvt Ltd, Noida ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and safeguards your personal information when you register for or attend The Future Technologies Summit 2026.

  1. Information We Collect
    2. When you register for the Summit, we may collect personal information including but not limited to your name, email address, phone number, job title, company name, and payment details. We may also collect non-personal information such as browser type and IP address through our website utilizing cookies.
  2. How We Use Your Information
    3. The information we collect is used primarily to process your registration, facilitate your access to the event, provide customer support, and send you important updates regarding the summit agenda, speakers, or logistical changes. With your consent, we may also use your email to send promotional materials about future events.
  3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
    4. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. However, we may share your information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website, processing payments, or organizing the event, provided that these parties agree to keep this information confidential. We may also release information when required to comply with the law.

By attending the event, you consent to your voice, name, and/or likeness being used without compensation in any and all media for marketing and promotional purposes by the organizers.
  5. Data Security
    6. We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
  6. Your Rights
    7. You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate data, or request the deletion of your personal information, subject to certain legal exceptions. To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@futuretechnologiessummit.com.
