Refund & Cancellation Policy

Thank you for booking tickets for Future Technologies Summit. Please read our refund and cancellation policy carefully before making a purchase.

1. Ticket Cancellation by Participant

Tickets once purchased are non-transferable. Cancellation requests must be submitted via email to support@futuretechnologiessummit.com.

  • Cancellations requested at least 7 days before the event date may be eligible for a partial refund.
  • No refund will be issued for cancellation requests made less than 7 days before the event.
  • No refund will be provided for no-shows.

2. Refund Processing

If eligible, refunds will be processed within 7–10 working days to the original payment method.

Payment gateway charges and transaction fees may be deducted from the refundable amount.

3. Event Cancellation or Postponement

If the event is cancelled by the organizers, participants will receive a full refund.

If the event is postponed, tickets will remain valid for the rescheduled date. Refund requests in such cases will be handled at the discretion of the organizers.

4. Force Majeure

The organizers are not responsible for cancellations or changes due to unforeseen circumstances including natural disasters, government restrictions, or other force majeure events.
