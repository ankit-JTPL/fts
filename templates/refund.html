Refund Policy

Thank you for registering for The Future Technologies Summit 2026. This Refund Policy outlines the terms and conditions under which refunds for tickets and sponsorships are processed. By purchasing a ticket, you agree to this policy.

  1. Ticket Purchases
    2. All ticket sales for The Future Technologies Summit 2026 are considered final. Unless explicitly stated otherwise in this policy or required by law, we do not offer refunds, cancellations, or exchanges for purchased tickets, regardless of attendance.
  2. Event Cancellation or Postponement
    3. If the event organizers (ABC pvt Ltd, Noida) cancel the event entirely and it is not rescheduled, registered attendees will be offered a full refund of their ticket purchase price. If the event is postponed or rescheduled, your ticket will automatically be transferred to the new dates. If you cannot attend the rescheduled dates, you may request a refund within 14 days of the postponement announcement.
  3. Ticket Transfers
    4. If you are unable to attend the summit, you may transfer your ticket to another individual. Ticket transfer requests must be submitted in writing to info@futuretechnologiessummit.com at least 7 days prior to the start of the event. Please include the original purchaser's details and the full details of the person receiving the transferred ticket.
  4. Force Majeure
    5. The organizers shall not be held responsible for any delay or failure in performance or cancellation of the event due to causes beyond their reasonable control, including but not limited to acts of God, war, terrorism, pandemics, strikes, or governmental regulations (Force Majeure). In such circumstances, no refunds will be issued, but the organizers will strive to provide alternative arrangements such as a virtual summit or rescheduled dates.
  5. Processing Refunds
    6. When a refund is approved by the organizers, it will be processed using the original method of payment. Please allow up to 14-21 business days for the refunded amount to reflect in your account. Processing fees or transaction charges incurred during the initial purchase are strictly non-refundable.
